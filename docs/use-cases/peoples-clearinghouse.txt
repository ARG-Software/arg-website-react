PEOPLE'S CLEARINGHOUSE

Industry: Fintech & Financial Inclusion
Client: AMUCSS / People's Clearinghouse (lacamara.mx)
Featured engagement: 3 Years
Services: Backend, Frontend Implementation, Mobile App Development, Payments Architecture, Interoperability, Live Demo Support

THE CHALLENGE

Mexico's community banking sector connects millions of people in rural areas to financial services — but those institutions have been running on infrastructure that can't interoperate with the broader payment network. People's Clearinghouse set out to change that: a community-owned payments ecosystem built on Mojaloop vNext, capable of linking community banks, savings coops, and rural merchants to national-scale payment switching. The platform needed to perform under real pressure — handling 1,000–2,000 requests per second while keeping transfers sub-second — and it needed to survive the most public test imaginable: a live demo at Interledger Summit 2025 in Mexico City, followed by real merchants selling real goods to attendees through the app.

WHAT ARG DELIVERED

ARG extended years of Mojaloop vNext expertise into a purpose-built clearinghouse for Mexico's social financial sector. The work spanned the full stack — from payment API engineering to mobile app flows to live demo preparation — in partnership with Cyberbones, which led the overall system architecture.

- Built PCH-specific switch and participant flows for community banks, savings coops, and regulated payment institutions
- Load-tested payment APIs under sustained cluster conditions, processing 1,000–2,000 requests per second with linear scalability
- Tuned lookup and quote workflows to 200–300 ms end-to-end, with ledger transfers averaging 770 ms
- Validated reliability through multi-million request test runs with zero functional errors
- Developed mobile app paths for account transfers, QR merchant payments, confirmations, and full transaction visibility
- Prepared the live demo environment used for the main-stage Interledger Summit presentation
- Supported the community showcase where rural merchants sold goods to attendees through the app using fictional money
- Hardened the end-to-end request path so ~100 first-time summit users could transact without a single transfer failure

THE RESULTS

At Interledger Summit 2025, People's Clearinghouse validated the exercised flow on stage and in the hands of rural merchants selling coffee and traditional goods through the app. Roughly 100 participants completed more than 1,000 fictional-money transfers with zero failures.

Key Metrics:
- Requests per second in sustained cluster load tests: 1,000–2,000
- Average ledger transfer time in load tests: 770 ms
- Live showcase transfers (zero failures): 1,000+

Behind the scenes, the APIs proved they can support national-scale transaction switching — the foundation Mexico's community banking network needs to move from pilot to production.
