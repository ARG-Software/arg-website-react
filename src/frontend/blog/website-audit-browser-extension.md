Your website looks great.

The design is clean. The images load. The buttons work. Everything seems fine.

But what if your visitors are waiting longer than they should? What if something is making your website less secure? Or what if it's quietly loading things nobody actually needs?

Here's the thing about websites: some of their biggest problems are the ones you can't see.

And you shouldn't need to be a developer to find them.

That's why we built Arg Architect.

## It started with a question we kept asking ourselves

At ARG, we spend a lot of time looking at websites. And we kept noticing the same thing.

A website could look perfectly fine on the outside, but behind the scenes, it is a different story.

Huge images slowing everything down. Security settings that had been overlooked. Third-party tools adding unnecessary weight. Things that had been there for months, sometimes years, without anyone noticing.

Finding these problems meant jumping between different tools, interpreting complicated reports, and sometimes sending information to external services.

We kept thinking: "Why is it so difficult to get a clear picture of what's happening on a website?"

So we built something for ourselves.

Something simple, practical, and private.

Then we thought, why not let everyone use it?

## Meet Arg Architect

Arg Architect is a free browser extension that gives your website a health check.

You enter a website address, run an audit, and get a report showing what's working well, what could be improved, and why it matters.

Think of it like taking your car for an inspection. Everything might feel fine while you're driving, but it's reassuring to know someone has checked what's happening under the hood.

Except this inspection is free, and it takes about two minutes to run a quick check.

It looks at eight areas of your website, including:

- **Security:** Are there weaknesses that could put your website or visitors at risk?
- **Performance:** Is your website making people wait unnecessarily?
- **Efficiency:** Is it downloading more than it needs?
- **Accessibility:** Can people with different needs use it comfortably?
- **AI readiness:** Can AI tools understand your website and find useful information?

It also examines page structure, file sizes, and how much your website depends on external services.

And here's our favorite part: it explains problems in language people can understand.

Instead of throwing mysterious error codes at you, it tells you what's wrong, why you should care, and what can be done about it.

You can even copy a finding and send it straight to your developer as a task.

## Three things we weren't willing to compromise on

While building Arg Architect, we made three decisions that mattered to us.

**1. Your data stays yours.**

Your audit results stay in your browser, on your device. Even the small inference model used for part of the analysis runs locally. Two optional external lookups are disabled unless you give permission.

**2. It respects your privacy.**

You know those annoying cookie banners?

When Arg Architect encounters one, it doesn't automatically click "Accept All." It chooses to reject optional cookies or accept only what's essential.

We thought it would be pretty ironic to build a tool that checks websites for problems while casually accepting every tracking cookie along the way.

**3. It doesn't pretend to know what it doesn't know.**

A website might load quickly one moment and slowly the next.

That's why Arg Architect can run multiple controlled tests and use the middle result, rather than trusting one lucky attempt.

And if something can't be measured properly, it says so. No invented results. No false reassurance.

## Your website has a new kind of visitor: AI

Here's something that wasn't on most people's minds a few years ago.

Today, it's not just humans visiting websites. AI assistants and automated tools are reading them too.

People are increasingly using AI to discover businesses, research products, and compare services.

But can these tools actually understand your website?

Arg Architect checks whether your site's information is accessible and understandable to AI systems and whether important details match what your pages really say.

It won't guarantee that AI will recommend your business. But it can help you understand whether your website is making things unnecessarily difficult.

And that feels like something worth knowing.

## FAQ

- **Is Arg Architect free?** Yes. It is a free browser extension and needs no account.
- **What does the website audit check?** Security, performance, efficiency, accessibility, and AI readiness, plus page structure, file sizes, and dependence on external services.
- **Does my data leave my device?** No. Audit results stay in your browser, and the small inference model used for part of the analysis runs locally. Two optional external lookups stay disabled unless you allow them.
- **Which browsers are supported?** Chrome, Edge, Brave, and Opera through the Chrome Web Store, and Firefox through Firefox Add-ons.
